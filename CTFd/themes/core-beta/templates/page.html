HCMUS-CTF REWIND

Relive the Best. Reignite the Challenge.

HCMUS-CTF REWIND is a Capture The Flag (CTF) event that brings back the most iconic and well-crafted challenges from previous CTF competitions hosted by the Computer Security Club - University of Science, VNU - HCM.

Whether you're revisiting your favorite puzzles or facing them for the first time, REWIND offers a curated selection of past challenges across key cybersecurity categories:

  • 🔐 Cryptography
  • 🕵️ Digital Forensics
  • 🐞 Binary Exploitation
  • 🕸️ Web Security
  • 🔍 Reverse Engineering

Perfect for both beginners and experienced players, this event provides a fun, pressure-free environment to practice, learn, and celebrate the legacy of HCMUS-CTF.

Eligibility

Only current HCMUS students (except organizers and challenge authors) are eligible to participate. Prize eligibility requires that all team members must be current HCMUS students.

Rules

  • You may use any tool to solve challenges. Open-source tools are encouraged.
  • Each person may only join one team.
  • No sharing of flags, hints, or solutions between teams.
  • Teams may consist of up to 4 HCMUS students.
  • All teams must register with a valid email (preferably school email).
  • Do not attack infrastructure or unrelated systems.
  • No brute-force attacks (e.g., dirbuster, nmap) unless explicitly allowed.
  • Using tools like ChatGPT or Google Gemini is allowed, but not other people.
  • Flag format: HCMUS-CTF{TEXT_HERE} unless specified otherwise.
  • No brute-force guessing of flags. Contact support if you're unsure.
  • Violations may result in disqualification. Organizer decisions are final.

Scoring

  • Each challenge has a point value.
  • Ranking is based on total points. Tie-breaker: first to reach top score (excluding trivial solves).
  • Dynamic scoring: challenges solved by many teams are worth less.
  • No time bonuses.
  • Winners will be contacted via the registered email.
  • Organizers may require write-ups from winning teams.
  • If an ineligible team wins, the next eligible team gets the prize.

Swags

  • 1st Place: T-Shirts
  • 2nd Place: Lanyards
  • 3rd Place: Stickers
